Made from waste

Why the studio exists

Material is never the problem. Access is.

Thika produces more usable waste in a week than the studio can carry in a year. Packaging, offcuts, sheet metal, sacks. What young artists here lack is not raw material or ideas, it is a bench, a mentor and a buyer.

So we built the missing part. The studio collects and sorts the material, trains the people who want to use it, and sells the results so the makers get paid.

What we work with

Sorted, cleaned, logged, cut

Everything that comes through the gate is weighed and recorded before it reaches a bench. What we cannot use goes on to recyclers who can.

  • Sheet metal
  • Seed packets
  • Maize meal sacks
  • Bottle tops
  • Timber offcuts
  • Fabric ends
  • Cardboard
  • Wire
  • Broken tiles
